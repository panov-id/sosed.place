# sosed — What changed in the documents

**Last updated: 16 September 2026**

Each new edition of the Terms, the Privacy Policy or the Community Guidelines is listed here, newest first, in a few plain lines. The documents themselves are the text that applies; this page only tells you where to look.

## Edition of 16 September 2026 (the Guidelines are dated 15 September)

**Terms**

- **The order of the feed is corrected** (§11): the newest messages are at the top, not at the bottom. The feed is still chronological, with no ranking; only the direction was described wrongly.
- **Age bands are described as they work** (§4): anyone 21 and over is never shown anyone under 19, and everyone under 19 is cut off from 21+; the bands meet at their edges. The text used to say the two sides never see each other.
- **Hangman** (§9, §11): the word set in a game of hangman is seen by our server and checked like a feed message, and a refused word counts towards the 15-minute pause.
- **Offers hidden by complaints** (§10, §11, §15): an offer is hidden automatically after complaints from three different people who were already posting in the feed before it came out — their first publication more than a day old; a link in an offer goes dark after reports from two such people.
- **Content involving minors** (§8): it is refused before publication and reported where we suspect a criminal offence threatening life or safety (§15), rather than promised to be reported in every case.
- **New editions** (§19): the Community Guidelines, too, wait for your checkbox inside the Service; nothing is recorded as accepted on your behalf.
- **Disputes** (§20): the reference to the EU online dispute resolution platform is removed.
- **Names** (§8): a refusal to accept a display name is named next to a refusal to publish — both automatic, both final.
- **One date** (§9): the game-board exception is dated 2026-09-09, the day it was decided, in all three documents.

**Privacy Policy**

- **New data named:** your appearance in the app — theme, contrast step and accent colour — is kept with your identity on our node, separately for each service you use it through, so that it moves with the identity (§3), on the same legal basis as the rest of the Service (§4); it lives as long as the identity and is deleted at once when you close it (§5), and the copy on your device is named among local storage (§12).
- **Hints:** which of the in-app hints you have closed is remembered in your browser's local storage, and starting over clears it (§12).
- **More data named** (§3): your session, the key share and the hashes of your PIN and recovery code; the detected language of a message; matches, blocks and reports; tables; what you accepted; business accounts and venues; the email required for a complaint about a discount; the early-access choice on the waitlist; and what is kept only on your device (§12).
- **Legal bases** (§4): each kind of data is placed under its ground in Art. 6 GDPR.
- **Retention** (§5): an identity with no live session for a year is closed; a closed identity is deleted after 30 days, and the paper code no longer brings it back; age is kept as a number of years; at most 200 messages wait for you in one conversation, and the oldest beyond that are dropped silently.
- **Hangman** (§5, §6a): the word set in hangman is part of the game's state and goes through moderation.
- **Processors and where data sits** (§7, §8): Hetzner, which hosts our server and database in Germany, is named; every kind of letter Resend delivers is listed, with the safeguards for that transfer; everything kept in Germany is listed.
- **Rights** (§9): the line about withdrawing consent is gone, because nothing we process rests on consent.
- **Children** (§10): the age bands are described as in the Terms.
- **New editions** (§13): as in the Terms.
- **More data named, again** (§3, §6a): the display name is required and moderated like a message; the languages you chose for the feed; the phrases you hid and the people you blocked, with the block's date; your support requests and our answers.
- **One date** (§6a): the game-board exception is dated 2026-09-09.

**Community Guidelines**

- **Hangman:** the word set in hangman is the one word our server sees and checks; a refused word counts towards the 15-minute pause.

## Edition of 14 September 2026

**Terms**

- **The posting limit is named** (§8): four messages per sliding hour.
- **What happens when the rules are broken** is now a closed list (§15): a message is not published; after five automated refusals within an hour nothing goes to checking for 15 minutes; content goes for everyone after enough reports or is removed after a notice of illegal content; a link in an offer goes dark; an offer is rejected or removed; a venue is suspended after repeated justified complaints. We no longer reserve closing anyone's access at our own discretion.
- **Automated decisions are named as such** (§11): a refusal to publish and the 15-minute pause are automatic and final; decisions on reports may be automatic, and every decision says whether automated means were involved.
- **A link in an offer** goes dark by itself after reports from people who were already posting before the offer came out; the business is emailed at that moment (§10).
- **Offers** (§10): the reservation that we may take down any offer at our discretion is gone; an offer is rejected or removed on the grounds that section names.
- **Reporting to the authorities** happens where we suspect a criminal offence threatening someone's life or safety (Art. 18 DSA), separately from the list above.
- **New editions** (§19): on the website you are told what changed; inside the Service, with an identity, you confirm a new edition with one checkbox.

**Privacy Policy**

- **New data named:** the moments of your latest publications and refusals and the date of your first publication (§3, §5); the "stepped away" label on your conversations (§5).
- **Support messages:** after you start over, the text and your email stay for the year as a record; a report sent through support moves to the notice register (§5).
- **Recipients:** law enforcement, judicial authorities or Europol, where Art. 18 DSA requires (§7).
- **A suspended venue:** a keyed hash of its address is kept one more year (§5).
- **Moderation** (§6a): the sentence inviting you to write to support so that a person looks at a refused message is gone — a refusal to publish is automatic and final, and support does not review it (Terms §11).
- **Analytics:** the last paragraphs about Google Analytics are gone, and so is its code (§12).
- **New editions** (§13): as in the Terms.

**Community Guidelines**

- **Consequences of violations** now list what actually happens, including passes and spectators at a table, instead of "a reduced quota or losing access".
